# Logo

Kai's mark is **ϗ**, the kai compendium (Unicode GREEK KAI SYMBOL, U+03D7):
the medieval Greek shorthand for καί, "and", Greek's ampersand. Its outline
comes from EB Garamond (SIL Open Font License); GFS Didot, the wordmark's
font, has no ϗ, and a browser would otherwise substitute whatever font it
has, so the mark is always a drawn path, never text.

- `kai-400.json`, `kai-600.json`: the glyph's outline (font units, y up)
  at weights 400 and 600.
- `site-assets.py` writes `public/favicon.svg` (gold ϗ on a dark rounded
  square) and `public/images/kai-mark.svg` (the bar's logo).
- `mockups.py` draws the logo ideas in `mockups/` (open
  `mockups/index.html`): ϗ alone, on a coin, in fine and bold meander
  frames, in circular meanders (fine, bold, and at a coin's rim), in an
  Antikythera gear (also inside a meander), the archer's
  kairos, and the wordmark over a meander band.
