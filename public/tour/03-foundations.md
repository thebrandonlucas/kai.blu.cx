# New Foundations

Kai aims to help computers live up to their potential, with intuitive,
friendly tools built on deterministic foundations.

Never again lose a day to
[dependency hell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dependency_hell), shrug that
it ["works on my machine"](https://blog.codinghorror.com/the-works-on-my-machine-certification-program/),
or put off an upgrade in case something breaks. And *enjoy* your setup again.

Countless hours go into these problems, and most fixes are half-baked or hard
to use, leaving you confused, demotivated, and still at risk by the end of it.
