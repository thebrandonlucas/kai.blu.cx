# Determinate Computing, Made Easy

[Nix](https://nixos.org) and [Guix](https://guix.gnu.org) made it possible.
Kai makes it practical.

- **One file:** `Kaifile.roc` describes your project's environments, shells,
  tasks, builds and workflows, in a small typed language.
- **A few commands:** `kai shell`, `kai run`, `kai build`, `kai workflow`.
- **Better defaults:** every tool is pinned and reproducible, without you
  asking.

> Intuitive, easy, and fun.

Press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to see the Kaifile run.
