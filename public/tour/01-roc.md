# Upon This Roc

Kai is both a Domain-Specific Language (DSL) and CLI tool built in
[Roc](https://www.roc-lang.org), designed to unlock the power of determinate
computing.

There are two determinate systems today: [Nix](https://nixos.org) and
[Guix](https://guix.gnu.org), but they are either hard to learn, difficult to
use (Nix), or limited (Guix). They are powerful and brilliant tools, but in
practice cause more problems than they solve for most people. What's the point
in having a revolutionary tool if no one wants to join the revolution?

Kai builds on top of them to make the benefits obvious, easy, and fun.
